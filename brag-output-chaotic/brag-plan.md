# Brag Plan: LIFT & COAST — Chaotic Cut

Second cut of the same project. The polished broadcast package lives in `brag-output/`;
this one is the loud, fast, over-caffeinated sibling.

## What is this app?

A 2026-regulation open-wheel formula racing game that runs entirely in a browser tab —
27 real circuits rebuilt from real GPS centerlines, on a terrain height field, with a
generated racing line that paints itself onto the tarmac.

## The angle

The polished cut argues. This one **shouts**. The product's absurd-but-true facts are
fired at the viewer one at a time, over the most crowded, highest-speed, most chaotic
frame from each circuit: a 20-car pack stacked onto a crimson braking zone in the rain,
traffic at Singapore, a car sideways at Las Vegas, Monaco's hairpin with a Ferrari
alongside.

Every cut carries the circuit's real name and real corner count in the app's own ticker
voice (`MONACO /// 19 CORNERS`), so the "27 circuits" claim is never just a number — it
is a name and a corner count you can check.

## Hook (first 1.4 seconds)

No logo card, no build-up. Flash in on the single most violent frame in the pool — a
dense pack of cars stacked onto a bright pink braking zone at Eau Rouge in the rain —
and slam **27 CIRCUITS.** at 200px. Red flash frame on the cut.

## Key moments (the middle)

Seven hard cuts, one claim each, no scene over 1.7 seconds:

1. `REAL GPS CENTERLINES.` over Monza
2. `NOT A FLAT PLANE.` over Baku's gradient — the README's own line: *"not on a flat
   plane with a pretty texture"*
3. `SO WHERE DO I LIFT?` over the Spa broadcast camera, answered by the four zone colors
   slamming in as chips
4. `20 CARS. ONE RACING LINE.` over a chase-cam pack with the timing tower stacking up
5. `CHANGE THE WEATHER MID-LAP.` over a hard 1.6x push into the live Race Ops panel in
   the rain
6. `STEWARDS SEE EVERYTHING.` over the cockpit camera with the red
   `TRACK LIMITS WARNING 2/3` banner
7. `IT RUNS IN A TAB.` over Singapore traffic

## Outro / punchline

`LIFT & COAST` slams in at 240px over Madrid's banked corner, tagline underneath, then
three one-word-per-beat hits stacked down the frame: **NO SERVER. / NO INSTALL. / NO
EXCUSES.** Then the real home hero for one beat, and a cut to black.

## Tone

- Preset: `chaotic`
- Creative direction: *overproduced mobile game ad — ALL CAPS, tilted words, red flash
  frames, one number per cut, no restraint whatsoever.*
- Interpretation: pacing comes from hard cuts and zoom cuts, never from yanking text
  off screen. Every line is 1–5 words and holds past its read floor. Confidence is
  volume, but the type is still big enough to read.

## Format: landscape — 2560x1440
## Duration: 16.93s

## Visual identity (from the project)

Unchanged from the polished cut — same palette, same faces, same four zone colors:
background `#040509`, accent `#e10600` (type-safe red `#ff3b2f`), gold `#d3ab63`, text
`#f4f1e8`, Archivo 900 italic display, Geist Mono for the circuit tags and data.
The chaotic register changes *scale and rhythm*, not the brand.

## Audio direction

- Role: dense rhythmic layer — this is the one tone that earns a loud mix.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (1:00, compact loop,
  punchy — the `chaotic` pick), volume 0.38, running the full 16.93s.
- Music treatment: hard in at 0.0, no fade-in, cut to silence with the last flash.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read — tempo 109.96 BPM. Every scene boundary is
  snapped to the beat grid (1.37, 2.46, 3.55, 5.19, 6.82, 8.22, 9.83, 11.47). Strong
  cues at **8.73s, 15.82s, 18.01s, 18.55s, 20.19s**; lock the Race Ops push to 6.82 and
  the `LIFT & COAST` slam to 11.47. The three `NO …` beats snap to 14.73 / 15.28 / 15.82.
- Audio-reactive treatment: expressive but not literal — bass energy drives a red glow
  behind the giant type and a slight scale breath on the wordmark. No waveform, no
  equalizer, no strobing type.
- SFX posture: dense and stacked, one hit per cut. Heavy punch on each hard cut, a
  glitch accent on the `SO WHERE DO I LIFT?` beat, a plate hit on the steward banner, and
  a deep bell on the outro slam. This is the one place a grab-bag is allowed.
- Audio-coupled moments: every scene cut, the four zone chips, the timing tower stack,
  the three `NO …` beats, lights-out.
- Restraint rule: none. That is the point of this cut.
